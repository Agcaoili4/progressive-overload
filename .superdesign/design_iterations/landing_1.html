You can already see a friend's workout. What you cannot do is finish the week and find out who actually did more — so the rivalry stays a running joke that quietly fizzles out.

A meet has a start, a finish and a result. That is the entire difference.

A week, start to finish

  1. 1

    Monday, everyone resets

    Last week's winner starts level with everyone else.

  2. 2

    Train and log as usual

    Your sets score themselves. Nothing extra to do.

  3. 3

    Sunday night, it resolves

    One winner, recorded. Then it starts again.

Finishing fifth means you lost this week — which is the point. Nobody is ever out of it.

The score is designed so that winning it means you trained well.

Most fitness scoring rewards whoever grinds hardest. This one has caps, and they are deliberate — if the best strategy for points were bad training, the scoring would be wrong.

Consistency

~40%

Showing up, with a ceiling

Your first four sessions carry the most. The fifth and sixth count for less. The seventh counts for nothing.

You cannot win by training ten times a week — which is neither healthy nor fair on anyone with a job.

Effort

~40%

Hard sets, not tonnage

A set counts when it is genuinely close to your own capacity — so warmups are excluded automatically, and a heavier person has no built-in advantage.

Capped per muscle group, so twenty sets of curls wins nothing.

Progress

~20%

A PR is a PR

Beating your own best pays a flat amount. Five pounds and two hundred pounds are worth exactly the same.

Which is also why lying is pointless: an invented number earns what an honest one earns.

Things this will never do

Punish you for resting

Declare a rest week and you sit it out — no score, no loss, nothing broken. Real programmes deload, and a system that penalises recovery is harming its users.

Sell anything that changes a result

No point boosts, no multipliers, no paid streak saves. The moment money moves a ranking, every ranking is meaningless.

Make you beat a stranger

Lobbies are private and joined by invite code. You compete with people you know, which is the only version of this anyone actually wants.

Reward the biggest person in the room

Effort is measured against your own capacity, not an absolute number. A beginner and a lifter of ten years can win the same week.
